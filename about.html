introduction

occasionally sourced is first and foremost a collaborative project, searching for the right sources to carry out sustainable translation of cultural contexts. Driven by the curiosity between the local and diaspora community in various locations, O.S invites artists, writers, translators, and publishers to join in on an open process of sharing their works to diversify the forms they can exist in.
O.S aims to move in the pedagogical corner of archival systems, focusing on grassroots research efforts and small communities, bringing localised stories to the front without the exclusive politics of travelling.
